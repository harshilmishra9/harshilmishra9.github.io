About me

I bridge manufacturing operations, industrial engineering and digital technology to improve productivity, compliance, visibility and business performance.

I’m an engineering professional with experience in pharmaceutical and biologics manufacturing and in manufacturing operations. I look at how work actually flows on the floor, find where a process loses time, quality or visibility, and then fix it with Lean Six Sigma, data analysis and, where it helps, a purpose-built digital tool.

That means working across five areas: manufacturing operations, operational excellence, industrial engineering, digital application development and data-driven problem-solving. I hold an M.Tech in Production Engineering from NIT Rourkela and a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering from SISTec, Bhopal.
